Selected work / mixed reality game

Wizard’s Way

Work in progress · More development details coming soon.

Become a wizard’s apprentice, cast elemental spells, and step through portals in your own walls.

My role

Game Developer · Dream Park

Focus

  • Modular environments & level design
  • Enemy combat & rune selection
  • Playful wand interactions

Wizard’s Way turns a physical room into a magic academy. As a wizard’s apprentice, players take quests from Froggid, a floating frog familiar, and step through portals into a werewolf’s study, a goblin swamp, an orc coliseum, and a spider dungeon.

Each encounter asks players to read elemental rune stones and choose the right spell. Defeating the creature earns a rune, bringing them closer to the final dragon battle. Between quests, players can experiment with potions, practice at the carnival, and uncover playful wand interactions around the academy.

01 / Level design

Magic that fits the room

Each encounter has two layouts: an open environment for larger rooms and a compact portal framed through one wall. The same adventure fits a living room or a full venue.

Learn the wand by playing

Players learn to aim and flick by exploring: plants turn into butterflies, a skull sneezes dust, and a box releases bats.

Practice at the carnival

Axe throwing, ball toss, and a shooting range offer practice between quests, with targets and props that restock.

Experiment at the cauldron

Discover a recipe by dropping ingredients into the cauldron. Each reaction signals whether the ingredient belongs.

02 / Enemy combat & rune selection

Match the rune, cast the spell

Rune stones hover above each enemy, showing which spell will deal damage. Players choose Fire, Lightning, or Freeze and cast with a wand gesture. Matching spells hit; others visibly deflect.

03 / Dragon boss battle

The dragon’s hoard

Collecting every rune unlocks the finale: a lava-lit cavern guarded by a dragon with sweeping fire-breath attacks.

Built on Dream Park’s existing assets, world, and systems.
